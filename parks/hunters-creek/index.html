TipAbout the Mud Model

Trail muddiness is estimated from a weighted composite of the top 9 cm of soil moisture (ERA5-Land reanalysis for history, Open-Meteo model for forecast days).

Scoring: A site-specific scale factor is applied to soil wetness, then a logistic sigmoid converts it to an internal 0–10 mud index (0 = bone dry, 10 = impassable). A precipitation boost applies when rainfall exceeds 5 mm in the prior 24 h. The displayed 1–10 trail condition score inverts the mud index (score = 11 − mud index), so 10 = Great conditions and 1 = Avoid.

Frozen: flagged when the 3-day rolling mean of shallow soil temperature (0–7 cm) drops to or below 0°C; single cold air-temperature spikes alone do not trigger it.

Calibration parameters (midpoint, steepness, precip_boost, soil_moisture_scale) are set per park in data/parks.yml. The model has not been validated against field observations — treat scores as estimates, especially near level boundaries.
