TipAbout the Mud Model

Trail muddiness is estimated using a weighted average of soil moisture in the top 9 cm (ERA5-Land reanalysis for history, Open-Meteo forecast model for future days). A logistic sigmoid maps soil wetness (0–0.6 m³/m³) to a 0–10 mud score. An additional boost is applied when precipitation exceeds 5 mm in the prior 24 hours. When surface temperature is below 0°C the trail surface is flagged as Frozen.

The model has not been calibrated against field observations at this location — treat mud scores as estimates, especially near the boundaries between named levels.
